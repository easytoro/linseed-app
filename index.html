Linseed

Oil paint, as it behaves.

Linseed is oil painting on the iPad. Blue and yellow make green. A loaded brush runs dry. Thick paint stands up in the light.

Support

Questions, bugs and ideas: zwdzkdesign@gmail.com. Say which iPad and which Pencil you have.

Testing the beta? Take a screenshot in Linseed and TestFlight offers to send it to me. That is the quickest way.

What you need

An iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. An Apple Pencil is best. A finger paints too, until a Pencil touches the canvas; then fingers move the view.

Your paintings

They stay on your iPad. Linseed keeps them as you paint, in its gallery. Save one as a file to move it or back it up.

Privacy

Linseed collects nothing. No account, no ads, no tracking. The privacy policy.
