Linseed support

Linseed is an oil painting app for iPad. It simulates real pigments, so colours mix the way they would on a palette, and thick paint keeps its texture.

Contact

If something's broken or you have a question, email me at zwdzkdesign@gmail.com. It helps to mention which iPad and Apple Pencil you're using.

If you're testing the beta, you can also send feedback through TestFlight. Take a screenshot while using Linseed and TestFlight will ask if you want to send it.

Requirements

Linseed needs an iPad running iPadOS 17 or later. An Apple Pencil is recommended, but you can paint with a finger too. Once you've used a Pencil, fingers switch to moving and zooming the canvas.

Saving your work

Paintings are saved on your iPad automatically as you work. To back one up or move it to another device, export it as a file.

Privacy

Linseed doesn't collect any data or connect to the internet. See the privacy policy for details.
