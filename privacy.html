Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Linseed is a painting app. It collects no personal data and has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your paintings, your palette of tubes, the paints you make in the lab and your settings are kept on your iPad or Mac, in the app's own storage. They leave it only when you save or share a file yourself, and then only to the place you choose.

What goes over the network

Nothing. Linseed makes no network requests.

Crash reports

If you have agreed to share analytics with Apple (Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements), Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to the developer. They say where in the app a crash happened, not what you painted or who you are.

During the beta

Beta testers get Linseed through Apple's TestFlight. TestFlight tells the developer when you install and open the beta, and passes on crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send, with your device model and iOS version. If you were invited by email, the developer sees that address. Apple's privacy policy covers TestFlight itself.

Children

Linseed collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.
